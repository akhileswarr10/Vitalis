# Onboard Guide: Bone Density and Muscle Atrophy Countermeasures

## Applies When
- Daily exercise session duration drops below 45 minutes or prescribed weekly countermeasure protocol compliance falls below 85%.
- Ultrasound or bone mineral density indicator shows progressive microgravity deconditioning.
- Crew member reports focal muscle soreness, joint stiffness, or reduction in isometric force output.

## Action Steps
1. Verify scheduled exercise session on the Advanced Resistive Exercise Device (ARED): perform prescribed axial loading movements including squats, deadlifts, and heel raises.
2. Ensure cylinder vacuum harness and footplate straps on ARED are calibrated to simulate 1-G ground reaction forces.
3. Complete aerobic conditioning on the Cycle Ergometer with Vibration Isolation and Stabilization System (CEVIS) or T2 Treadmill with bungee harness load set to ≥70% body weight.
4. Distribute physical training evenly across the mission day: pair morning resistive axial loading with afternoon cardiovascular conditioning.
5. Inspect foot placement and harness padding to prevent localized pressure sores and nerve impingement during high-load reps.
6. Consume post-workout high-protein dietary rations and ensure hydration levels meet mission countermeasure specifications.
7. Record completed reps, load settings, duration, and subjective perceived exertion in the daily countermeasure log.

## Escalation Criteria
- Total daily exercise countermeasure completion falls below 20 minutes for two consecutive days.
- Acute musculoskeletal injury, joint effusion, or persistent severe spinal pain following loading maneuvers.
- Inability to perform emergency egress mobility requirements due to muscle weakness.
- Escalate immediately to crew medical officer and ground exercise physiologist.
